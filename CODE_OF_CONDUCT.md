# Code of conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, professional, and constructive;
to avoid harassment, discrimination, and retaliation; and to protect
confidential information and security-sensitive details.

Do not post sensitive conduct reports in public issues or discussions. Report
them privately at
[masugate.governance@gmail.com](mailto:masugate.governance@gmail.com).
Security reports belong under the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
